# Model Card: LandShield Advisory Anomaly Classifier

## 1. Model Details
- **Model Name:** LandShield ML Advisory Risk Classifier
- **Architecture:** Gradient Boosted Decision Trees (`LightGBMClassifier` with balanced class weights) supported by an unsupervised baseline (`IsolationForest`).
- **Explainability Engine:** TreeSHAP (`shap.TreeExplainer`) with bilingual (Bangla/English) feature attribution mappings.
- **Version:** 1.0.0
- **Model Artifact Size:** ~0.41 MB (`backend/app/ml/model.joblib`), strictly below the 10 MB operational limit.
- **License / Usage:** Open-source Hackathon prototype for LandTech 2026. Strictly advisory.

---

## 2. Intended Use & Operational Boundary
- **Primary Use:** Providing registrar and land revenue officers with a second-opinion advisory risk score and top-3 SHAP feature explanations on land deed transactions.
- **Strict Invariants:**
  1. **Non-Decisive Advisory Only:** The ML layer cannot block registration or set `CRITICAL` or `BLOCKED` status.
  2. **Non-Overriding:** The ML model can never lower a deterministic risk score or dismiss a rule violation.
  3. **Capped Risk Elevation:** When ML anomaly probability $\ge 0.85$, it may elevate an advisory score by at most one tier (e.g., `LOW` $\to$ `MEDIUM`, or `MEDIUM` $\to$ `HIGH`).
  4. **Graceful Bypass:** If the model artifact is missing or corrupted, the system logs a warning and proceeds with deterministic rule evaluation without disruption.

---

## 3. Training & Validation Data
- **Synthetic Origin:** 100% synthetic dataset generated by `backend/ml/gen_dataset.py`. Zero real-world citizen names, national IDs, or government records.
- **Volume:** 1,898 total synthetic transfer records spanning 600 unique plots.
- **Data Partitioning & Leakage Prevention:**
  - 80% Train (`split == 'train'`) / 20% Test (`split == 'test'`).
  - Partitioned strictly by `plot_id` (`plot_id % 5 == 0` for test, remainder for train). No plot's transactions ever span across both train and test splits, preventing temporal or plot-level data leakage.
- **Anomaly Types Injected:**
  1. Circular Wash Trading ($A \to B \to C \to A$ cycle within 60 days).
  2. Off-Hour Collusion (transactions submitted at unusual hours, e.g., 2:30 AM).
  3. Price Collapse / Under-declaration (deed price $< 25\%$ of historical area median).
  4. Near-Miss Control Cases (legitimate intra-family concession transfers at 70% median, rapid commercial re-sales).

## 5. Performance Evaluation (Test Split Benchmark)
Evaluated on 381 unseen test transactions across 120 isolated test plots:

| Strategy | Precision | Recall | F1 Score | PR-AUC | False Positive Rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Rules-Only** | 0.5000 | 0.0667 | 0.1176 | — | 0.0027 |
| **ML-Only (LightGBM)** | **1.0000** | **1.0000** | **1.0000** | **1.0000** | **0.0000** |
| **Hybrid (Rules + ML $\ge 0.85$)** | 0.9375 | **1.0000** | **0.9677** | — | 0.0027 |

*Finding:* Static deterministic rules detect double-sales and chain tampering with near-zero false alarms, but miss non-rule statistical patterns (circular transactions, off-hour filings, price anomalies). The advisory ML model captures these patterns with high precision, and the hybrid combination achieves 100% recall while preserving rule explainability.

## 6. Limitations & Ethical Considerations
- **Synthetic Data Generalization:** Model weights reflect simulated distributions. In production deployments, retrain with empirical institutional data under supervision.
- **Fair Lending & Anti-Bias:** The model uses zero demographic features (no gender, religion, ethnicity, or location proxy variables). Only transaction velocity, pricing deviation, and chain consistency metrics are consumed.
- **Human In The Loop:** Registration officers make all final determinations; the ML system merely surfaces top-3 risk indicators with SHAP explanations.
